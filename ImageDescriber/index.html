Frequently Asked Questions

Is this really free?

Yes, completely free with no signup or hidden fees. The AI model runs in your browser.

What happens to my images?

Nothing. They are processed entirely on your device and never uploaded or stored.

Why is the first description slow?

The AI model (~200MB) downloads once and is cached. After that, descriptions are fast.

How accurate are the descriptions?

The model works well for common scenes and objects. For critical use (legal alt text, etc.), review and edit the output.
